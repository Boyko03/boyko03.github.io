Fast-paced 2-4 player couch versus platform fighter

Project Overview

This is my first team project at BUas. It was made in the last block of my first year. In this project I was a Lead programmer, but my biggest contributions were in the gameplay:

Player States

I implemented the player's "walking", charging, and launched states.

- Walking State

One part of the player is anchored while the other one is spinning around it. When the player presses any of the face buttons, if the spinning part can anchor itself on a platform, the other one starts spinning around it.

- Charging State

The player starts to spin faster. The player can choose in which direction they will be launched which is indicated with an arrow.

- Launched State

The player transforms into a ball and starts falling. Example in the following section.

Teleports and Flippers

Teleports consist of two parts whose positions can be adjusted in the level editor:

The flippers are located at the bottom of the level and they launch the player in a certain direction if it hits them.

Triangle Bumpers

The player bounces of the bumpers. The triangle bumper works in a similar way as the circular one, but its shape is a triangle (see above video).
